Is your metabolism responding—or stuck in one gear?

FOX2 looks at two things: how strong your fat-use signal is across the day, and whether that signal moves. Together, they can help you see when your metabolism appears responsive, when progress may have stalled, and when a persistent pattern deserves a closer look.

Your daily level is only half the picture.

FOX2 shows whether your daily fat-use signal was low, medium, or high. It also looks for meaningful movement between readings, distinguishing a steady day from a responsive one.

How it works

A breath signal becomes a useful daily picture.

As your body draws more energy from fat, your liver produces acetone that leaves in your breath. FOX2 reads that signal, accumulates the day, and shows both your overall level and the movement between readings.

Fuel Signal

Each breath provides a snapshot of your current fat-use signal. A single reading is one moment, not the verdict on your metabolism.

Using FOX2

Every breath adds another point to the Daily Fuel Score. There is no rigid schedule to follow.

Your live score shows progress toward a completed daily result; it is not meant to be judged one moment at a time.

03

What FOX2 tells you—and what it doesn’t.

FOX2 uses a breath-derived signal to estimate fat use. It does not directly measure insulin, resting metabolic rate, muscle loss, calories, or grams of body fat burned.

Its patterns are prompts for attention—not medical diagnoses. A higher score is not automatically better; the useful information is the combination of level, movement, and direction over time.
