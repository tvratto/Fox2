How to measure

A FOX2 measurement takes about a minute. Follow the prompts on the device.

FOX2
Measure
Data
Settings

Turn on and start

Hold the white button for two seconds. Use the orange button to highlight Measure, then press the white button.

During the measurement
1

Warm up

Wait through the countdown and breathe normally.

2

Take a deep breath and blow

Blow steadily and completely through the mouthpiece—like blowing out birthday candles—until FOX2 buzzes or you run out of air.

3

See your result

FOX2 shows your current Fat Use Zone, then updates your daily pattern and Fat Use Score.

Before you measure

Get a clean reading

  • Wait an hour after food or flavored drinks, or rinse your mouth thoroughly first. Plain water is fine.
  • Wait at least 15 minutes after exercise or another measurement.
  • Alcohol can interfere. If you drink, wait until the next day to measure.
